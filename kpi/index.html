BSNL L1 Field In-Charge Responsibilities

  • Site Power & Infra: Maintain EB supply, DG set, SMPS rectifiers, and battery backup to prevent Availability drops (<98%).
  • Backhaul Media (OFC / DMW): Check OFC patch cords, CPAN/MPLS ports, and microwave link alignment when IP Latency (>60ms) or VoLTE Packet Loss (>1.5%) occurs.
  • Physical RF & Compass Audit: Inspect jumper connectors, weatherproofing, physical antenna tilt/azimuth, and verify crossed feeders on swap alerts.
  • Static Data Completion: Measure and submit missing Lat/Long, Azimuth, Height, and Tilt for sites flagged in the GIS Audit drawer.

TCS / Tejas Team Responsibilities

  • Actionable Zero-GB (>98% Avail): Execute remote Cell Lock/Unlock, S1-U/PGW check, or eNodeB reset for non-halted sites carrying ≤5 MB with >98% availability.
  • Tejas RAN Hardware (BBU / RRU / SFP): Diagnose CPRI optical alarms and replace faulty RRUs, BBUs, or SFPs under AMC/Project scope.
  • RF Optimization & Neighbor Tuning: Define missing B01 ↔ B28 neighbor relations (ANR) and fix PCI clashes when Handover SR (<92%) degrades.
  • Capacity & Tilt Balancing: Adjust electrical tilt (RET) and handover offsets (CIO) to relieve PRB Congested (>80%) sectors.
