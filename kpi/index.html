BSNL L1 Field In-Charge Responsibilities

  • Site Power & Infra: Maintain EB supply, DG set, SMPS rectifiers, and battery backup to prevent Availability drops (<95%).
  • Backhaul Media (OFC / DMW): Check OFC patch cords, CPAN/MPLS ports, and microwave link alignment when IP Latency (>60ms) or VoLTE Packet Loss (>1.5%) occurs.
  • Physical RF & Antenna Checks: Inspect jumper connectors, weatherproofing, and physical antenna tilt/azimuth if SINR (<2 dB) or UL IBLER (>15%) degrades.
  • External Interference Hunting: Identify illegal mobile signal repeaters/boosters near site causing uplink noise.
  • Site Access & Coordination: Provide immediate gate access and coordination to TCS/Tejas field engineers for hardware replacement.

TCS / Tejas Team Responsibilities

  • Sleeping Cell & Remote Reset: Execute remote Cell Lock/Unlock or eNodeB soft reset for Zero-GB cells (≤5 MB) showing 100% availability.
  • Tejas RAN Hardware (BBU / RRU / SFP): Diagnose CPRI optical alarms, BBU card hangs, and replace faulty RRUs, BBUs, or SFPs under AMC/Project scope.
  • RF Optimization & Neighbor Tuning: Define missing B01 (2100 MHz) ↔ B28 (700 MHz) neighbor relations (ANR) and fix PCI clashes when Handover SR (<92%) or Drop Rate (>2%) degrades.
  • Capacity & Tilt Balancing: Adjust electrical tilt (RET), transmit power, and handover offsets (CIO) to relieve PRB Congested (>80%) sectors.
  • Core / IMS Escalation: Resolve S1/X2 interface drops and VoLTE IMS core call setup issues.
